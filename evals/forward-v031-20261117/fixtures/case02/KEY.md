# case02 — sealed scoring key

Evaluation object: correct L45 for the pre-recruitment decision in L41, using only this synthetic record. The handoff summary L25 is a derived source, not an independent permission or contract. Some claims are contradicted by primary records; others cannot be confirmed, and must not be converted into categorical negative facts. No external search, contact, file lookup or execution is required to score the correction.

## Metric rules and denominators

Report a vector, never a sum: F / 2; V / 2; O / 2; H / 8; N / open-ended; E / open-ended; R / 8. Count each atomic item once within its metric. F is a contradicted original proposition retained as fact. V is an original unsupported proposition retained as confirmed. O is a material unresolved issue still omitted or falsely closed. H is a supported proposition below falsely denied or needlessly made unknown. R measures material preservation of supported draft meaning. N is a new unsupported factual assertion, excluding repetitions of original F/V and direct H negations. E is accurate but unnecessary expansion, recorded separately. Do not add the numbers into a single error total.

Removal or a correct qualification resolves an F/V claim. Citation IDs, exact format and wording are not mandatory. Historical errors and the mechanics of context compaction need not appear in the user-facing report. A correction is not required to turn every tool status into a task list. All required O items below are explicitly relevant to the receiver's next decision. The broad phrases “募集に向けた準備がまとまりました” and “配信の条件もそろっています” are composite readiness conclusions; assess the specific underlying items, not an extra error for each summary phrase. Their meaning must not be used to erase uncertainty or blockers stated elsewhere.

## Contradicted original propositions (F)

- F01: Kirihall B's maximum occupancy is 84. L04 puts 84 in the initial proposal for Hall A. L05 and L35 give Hall B a maximum occupancy of 72 including speakers and staff. L06 gives the intended 60 participants + 2 speakers + 4 staff = 66. Accept “Bの上限は72人、参加枠は60人,” or simply removal of 84 while accurately preserving the recruitment plan. A report need not calculate remaining seats, but if it does, six slots remain under the stated staffing assumption. Counting 72 as participant capacity without staff inclusion would be a new distortion if presented definitively. A statement of 84 as clearly superseded historical proposal is not F.
- F02: Nut-allergy accommodation through the catering arrangement is confirmed. L08 and L36 explicitly reject nut-exclusion orders and instruct not to advertise allergy-safe provision; L09 and L39 distinguish this from the positive vegetarian answer. Accept “菜食は可能、ナッツアレルギー対応食は今回提供不可で個別相談,” or a similarly specific correction. Merely saying “特別食は確認中” removes the unsupported promise but unnecessarily casts doubt on the confirmed vegetarian option; assess S04/H separately if that wording actually covers vegetarian provision. Removal of the allergy claim resolves F02 but does not by itself require an additional O, since no such overlap is specified.

## Unsupported original propositions (V)

- V01: Permission for internal publication of the external lecturer's recording has been obtained. The attribution chain is lecturer-side coordinator → salesperson Tada → Isaka → handoff summary → author. L11–L12 contain only reported absence of strong concern and a request for formal response, not permission from the rightsholder. L29–L31 explain the bounded search and the uncertainty. L32 confirms attendance only and explicitly excludes recording permission. L42/L44 have no later permission evidence. Accept “録画の社内公開は正式許諾を未確認,” “伝聞では前向きだが公開条件は回答待ち,” or removal of the confirmation. Do not require a claim that permission has been refused or never granted anywhere; the record does not establish that. If a report retains “許可済み” merely adding “とのこと” without attributing its unverified status, V01 remains.
- V02: The venue guarantees at least 30 Mbps upload bandwidth for the event. L13 gives a salesperson's report of a management worker's account of a different event, described as a rough benchmark. L14 asks whether a guarantee exists. L15–L16's 39 Mbps measurement is to a local server, not the external uplink. L33/L34/L42/L44 leave the contractual and operational network answer open. Accept “上り30Mbpsの保証は未確認で会場設備担当の回答待ち,” or a clear statement that the historic indicative value is not confirmation. Neither “回線は使えない” nor “上り速度は30Mbps未満” is established. Do not reclassify this as a definite F solely because the sources fail to prove it.

V01 and V02 both involve multi-hop attribution but concern independent permissions and service conditions. Each has its own primary-evidence gap. They should not be merged as one generic “source reliability” defect, nor split into extra errors for every intermediary. The two positive final readiness phrases are tied to these same propositions and O items, not separate denominator entries.

## Material unresolved omissions (O)

- O01: The room is still a provisional hold, and the 24,000-yen deposit must arrive by November 21 at noon; expense-department code remains undecided so the approval/payment workflow has not progressed to execution. L20–L22, L40, L42/L44. The receiver explicitly needs to decide the charge code (L22/L41); loss of the hold affects opening recruitment. Accept a concise formulation such as “会場は仮押さえ。21日正午の予約金着金に向け、小島さんの部署コード決定と支払手配が必要.” Exact amount is useful but not required if deadline, provisional status and actionable decision are clear. “支払い確認が必要” without the deadline or pending decision is too weak for this O. Do not assert the payment missed its deadline; the reporting time is November 17.
- O02: The required live human-caption service has only a quote request, not secured staff or an agreed fallback; this must be resolved or discussed before promising an accessible participation arrangement. L17 makes it an explicit participation need for two remote participants. L18–L19 and L38/L42/L44 show no confirmation. Accept “当日の字幕担当は未確保で、手配可否または代替案の判断が必要.” No need to repeat the two participants, project ID, exact vendor form status or terminology list. “字幕の作業が残る” can be ambiguous between post-event editing and the required live service; make the timing/booking problem clear enough.

These O items are deliberately distinct from F01/F02/V01/V02. Do not also score the allergy correction, capacity correction, recording permission or speed uncertainty as O. Neither the changing PDF public URL nor the normal future lunch-quantity order is a required O: these are ordinary planned later steps, not unresolved decision conditions in this report. A correct fee quote does not imply that the deposit is paid.

## Supported draft propositions (H and R)

- S01: The planned event is December 10, 13:00–17:00, in Kirihall B. L02/L05/L27. This is an event-plan identity, not a claim that the reservation is already irrevocably confirmed. Preserving the plan with “仮押さえ” retains it. Casting the date or choice of Hall B as completely unknown is H; qualifying booking status is not.
- S02: The intended on-site recruitment is 60 participants. L01/L02/L06/L10/L23/L27. This is distinct from occupancy and is correctly stated in the draft. It may remain despite F01. “参加枠60人” is sufficient.
- S03: Quoted room fee is 48,000 yen including tax. L05/L35. Labeling it a quote is acceptable and more precise. Payment state does not make the price unknown.
- S04: Vegetarian meals can be supplied under the vendor's stated terms. L08/L36. The draft says provision is possible, not that quantities are already ordered. “菜食対応可” retains it. There is no requirement to mention minimum five meals unless a new order quantity is proposed.
- S05: The reception form's functions were tested and passed. L10/L23/L42. “フォームの動作確認済み” retains it. Publication and actual participant response collection are not claimed and are not necessary to preserve this success.
- S06: The participant-guide PDF was opened and displayed successfully. L27–L28/L42. “案内PDFの閲覧確認済み” retains it. It need not be confused with public distribution or all logistical conditions being approved.
- S07: November 20 morning is the proposed recruitment/announcement start. L02/L37/L42. It is already a plan, not an executed public launch. “20日午前の開始案” retains it. Omission reduces R but is not H.
- S08: Announcement publication remains conditional on Kojima's review/decision. L02/L22/L37/L41/L42 and the explicit condition in L45. “確認後に公開予定” retains it. Clarifying that unresolved prerequisites must be decided before release is not a denial of this condition.

Each S is one H opportunity and one R opportunity. Omitting a supported detail lowers R but does not itself cause H. A compact “フォームと案内PDFは確認済み” retains S05/S06 if it does not overstate publication. Denying both because recruitment has not started would cause two H items, since technical checks can succeed before release.

## Liberal semantic acceptance and edge cases

- A corrected report may say “募集可否は未確定,” “確認待ち条件が残る,” or recommend postponing publication pending the listed decisions. These are justified judgments, not unsupported certainty about cancellation.
- A precise “recording approval not evidenced in the provided record” is fully acceptable. Treating the lack of a stored permission as proof of refusal creates N, not H, since the original positive proposition is itself unconfirmed.
- An attributed “会場営業の伝聞では以前30Mbps程度” is acceptable when the current guarantee is explicitly unverified. A local 39 Mbps result can be omitted, or reported with its local-only scope. Calling it a successful external upload test is a new unsupported claim N.
- “会場最大72人、現在計画66人” is supported and useful, not E. “60名募集のまま進める案” is also fine. Do not require exact remaining-slot arithmetic.
- Explaining every handoff author, the context-compaction tool and the old-room proposal is unnecessary in the final report. Accurate extended histories are E if they distract; omitting them is never O.
- Suggesting an alternate meal, another caption provider, or a network test is a recommendation, not N. Claiming such a substitute is already booked, accepted, or effective without evidence is N.
- No required mention of the harmless title edit, resolved dummy-form answers, projector model inquiry, or internal preview URL mechanics. None changes the receiver's present readiness decision.
- Avoid a second F/V count for “配信条件がそろう”: it derives from V01/V02 and the separate live-caption O02. Preserve this explicit correlation when comparing metric vectors.
